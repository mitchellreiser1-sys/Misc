NURSING-HOME DATABASE PUBLIC-SOURCE REVIEW
As of 2026-10-30

RESULTS
3983 field-review rows cover 2710 unique facilities across 53 jurisdictions. All 407 verification-sample facilities and 2356 gap-list facilities are included; 53 facilities occur in both inputs.

Sample administrator verdicts: 290 CONFIRMED; 49 CHANGED; 52 UNVERIFIABLE; 16 SOURCE_ERROR. Source errors are a distinct evidence-misattribution category, not confirmed replacements.

Gap-list administrator review: 1161 supported / 1195 unresolved. Of the supported gap-list records, 785 were truly blank input names; the rest were low-confidence existing names confirmed or replaced. An accepted proposal may confirm an existing person rather than add a new name.

All blank operator/direct-organizational-owner fields in the sample/gap union were reviewed: 1178 field checks; 126 supported values and 1052 without a proposed value. These are field counts, not additional facilities. 96 operator/chain spot-check fields across 48 sample facilities are retained (requested minimum 40 facilities). 14 of those chain checks confirm only a blank published CMS field.

HOW TO READ STATE_SUMMARY.CSV
- rows_checked: unique facilities in the sample/gap union, not CSV field rows.
- confirmed and changed: final administrator verdicts for the verification sample only.
- unverifiable: verification-sample UNVERIFIABLE plus SOURCE_ERROR. Detailed separate counts and concrete source-error CCNs are in validation.json.
- gaps_filled and gaps_still_open: unique gap-list facilities with and without an accepted administrator proposal. Low-confidence nonblank names are included; these are not all new-name additions.
- best_public_source/source_url: most useful source found for administrator verification in that jurisdiction. Facility-specific examples are not presented as statewide administrator rosters; absence of a current named source is stated.

MERGE AND EVIDENCE POLICY
The final template columns match the supplied template exactly. Original CCNs, facility names, states and current values are preserved. Import the CCN column as Text in spreadsheet software to retain leading zeros and suffixes. 35 overlapping sample/remaining administrator reviews were reconciled in favor of the stronger final conservative review, with required sample verdicts and dated source evidence retained. No original values differed between overlapping sample and gap inputs.93 sample-only missing organization field reviews were added; 13 supported legal-entity/direct-owner values were independently rechecked against live exact-CCN CMS API responses, while 5 legal-name sentinels were explicitly rejected.

Historical-only evidence without current role corroboration is retained in notes, not proposed as current. 2025 license approvals covering an active 2026 license term are distinguished from stale dated correspondence. Current undated official/association pages retain access dates and appropriate medium-confidence caveats. Month-only dates preserve the source precision; no day is invented. CMS processing dates remain 2026-10-01.

Blank proposed_value means no supported proposal, not an instruction to erase a current value. A blank organization field may be legitimate where CMS reports only individual owners or does not disclose a legal entity. Source URLs can be dynamic portals; reproducible facility IDs and exact identifiers are retained in notes. Dated archived/indexed evidence and retrieval limitations remain explicitly labeled.

No LinkedIn source URLs are accepted in the final file. No facilities or individuals were contacted, no account-gated evidence was used, and no repository files were modified. This is a bounded public-source audit, not a guarantee of real-time employment or proof that no further public evidence exists.

findings.csv contains 2,505 input records, preserving every input identifier and the required column layout. Totals: 79 gap rows filled and 1,015 open; 522 medium rows confirmed, 20 changed, and 869 unverifiable. The Illinois listing is a complete capture of 4,485 search-result cards but only a partial export of profile fields. It is NOT 4,485 unique current facilities. See illinois_coverage.txt and illinois_source_evidence.zip before using it. Blank profile fields are missing extraction, not necessarily missing from the portal.

Unresolved outcomes reflect the sources and searches used, not proof no other evidence exists. Source dates shown are publication dates where available; otherwise dates viewed. Some older and undated pages require caution. The state summary's best source is representative of sources actually yielding results, rather than an assertion of a complete state roster; states with no verified proposals have blank source fields.

Systematic issues (examples):
1. Generic STATE_XX provenance in round 2 can mask round 1 facility-site sourcing, risking false independent confirmations. Example 555431; original was operator leadership website.
2. Title inflation: executive director, CEO, assistant administrator, and owner are not automatically the licensed administrator. Example 225762: Edward Forfa is executive director while Chanel Hayes is named administrator.
3. Synthetic, extended, or duplicate identifiers appear in the CCN field. Example 245583A and 335287001; identifiers are preserved verbatim and should not be normalized automatically. 225293 and 225293A appear to be duplicate facilities.
4. State fields can merge administrator with other roles. Example 245470A Minnesota field combines administrator/authorized agent; the named person is also described as a corporate executive, so it was not taken alone as administrator proof.
5. Facility sources disagree or are undated; avoid assuming a change without a chronology. Example 325042, conflicting administrator names from different sources, left unverifiable.
6. Illinois portal records include inactive, closed and pre/post ownership-change duplicates. Example facility ID 6012678, linked to 145029, appears under multiple facility names. Review Active status and evidence before ingestion.
7. Placeholder values can be mistaken for people. Example 355093, 'Administrator Interim'.
